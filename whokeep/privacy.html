Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WhoKeep handles information when you use the application.

1. Information used by the app

WhoKeep helps you keep track of the people you call while choosing someone, for example a contractor, a tutor, or a place to rent. The app does not ask for your name, email address, account, or any other information about you.

The app keeps only the information you enter:

  • your projects: their names, icons, and whether they are archived;
  • the contacts you add to a project: a name, a phone number, where you found them (for example Google, Instagram, or a friend), and their status, such as "To call", "Waiting", or "Done";
  • the history of each contact: the outcomes of your calls that you record ("Talked", "No answer", and so on) and your notes;
  • the follow-up reminders you set;
  • your settings, such as the reminder time, the default follow-up, and whether the app should ask how a call went.

The contacts you add are usually other people. Please add only the information you need to make your choice, and avoid writing sensitive information in notes.

2. Local storage

All of this information is stored locally on your iPhone. WhoKeep does not operate servers, does not have user accounts, and does not upload this or any other information to the developer.

Depending on your device settings, locally stored app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone. These backups are managed by Apple according to its own terms and your settings, not by WhoKeep.

3. Phone calls

When you tap a call button, WhoKeep opens the iPhone Phone app with the contact's number. The call itself is made by iOS. WhoKeep does not record calls, does not have access to your call history, and does not know how long a call lasted. After you return to the app, it may ask how the call went; your answer is saved only if you choose to save it.

4. Caller ID

If you turn on WhoKeep in Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification, the app gives iOS the names and phone numbers of the contacts in your projects, so that the name of the person and the project is shown when they call you. This list is handed to iOS on your device and is not sent anywhere else. WhoKeep is not told who calls you. You can turn Caller ID off at any time in the same iOS settings.

5. Your Contacts

When you add a contact with "Choose from Contacts", iOS shows its own contact picker. WhoKeep receives only the name and phone number of the one contact you pick and does not have access to the rest of your address book.

When you mark someone as chosen, you can tap "Save to Contacts". iOS then opens its own "New Contact" screen, filled in with that person's name and phone number. The contact is added to your iPhone Contacts only if you tap "Done" there, and you can edit or delete it in the Contacts app like any other contact. Your notes and call history are not copied to Contacts. Apart from this, WhoKeep never adds, changes, or deletes anything in your iPhone Contacts.

6. Notifications

Follow-up reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. You can allow or turn off notifications for WhoKeep in the iOS Settings app.

7. Purchases

WhoKeep Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details, name, or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the purchase has been made.

8. Exporting your data

If you export a project as a spreadsheet (CSV), the file is created on your device and passed only to the app or place you choose in the iOS share menu. What happens to it after that is governed by that app. WhoKeep does not share anything unless you choose to export.

9. Network access, analytics, and tracking

WhoKeep's features work offline. Apart from in-app purchases, which are handled by Apple, the app does not connect to the internet.

WhoKeep does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other third-party SDKs that collect usage information. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across other apps or websites.

WhoKeep does not sell or share personal information.

10. Children's privacy

WhoKeep does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Data retention and deletion

Your information stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete a single contact (swipe left on it in the project, or use "Delete contact" when editing it) or a whole project together with its contacts. Deleting a contact or a project also removes its reminders and removes its numbers from Caller ID.

All app data is removed when you uninstall WhoKeep. Copies included in iPhone backups are managed through your Apple backup settings.

12. Security

Because your information never leaves your iPhone through the app, it is protected by your device's own security features, such as the passcode and the operating system's app data isolation.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WhoKeep's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the WhoKeep developer at car.tools.apps@gmail.com or using the contact information provided on the application's App Store listing.
