WhoKeep Support

Need help with WhoKeep? Try the answers below or contact the developer.

How does WhoKeep work?

Create a project for whatever you are choosing, for example "Roof repair" or "Kitchen furniture". Add the people you plan to call, by typing a name and number or with "Choose from Contacts". Tap the green call button to call someone; when you come back, WhoKeep asks how it went, so you can save the outcome, a note, and a follow-up date. The project shows who still needs a call, who you are waiting on, who you talked to, and who you chose.

Follow-up reminders

When you set a follow-up, WhoKeep reminds you with a notification on that day. You can change the time reminders arrive in Settings. If reminders do not appear, check that notifications are allowed for WhoKeep in the iOS Settings app and that a Focus mode is not hiding them. In the Follow-ups tab, swipe left on a reminder to mark it done or remove it.

Seeing who is calling (Caller ID)

WhoKeep can show the name of the person and the project when someone from your projects calls you, even if they are not in your iPhone Contacts. To turn it on, open the iOS Settings app, go to Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification, and switch on WhoKeep. If a number is already saved in your iPhone Contacts, iOS shows the name from Contacts instead.

Saving the person you chose to Contacts

People you add to WhoKeep are not added to your iPhone Contacts, so your address book stays clean while you are still choosing. Once you mark someone as chosen, open them and tap "Save to Contacts": iOS shows its "New Contact" screen with the name and number filled in, and you can edit them before tapping "Done". Notes and call history stay in WhoKeep.

Archiving a project

When a job is done, archive its project from the project's menu. An archived project is kept for reference only: you can open it and its contacts, but not call, edit, add notes or set reminders, and its reminders are paused. Calls from its contacts are still recognized by Caller ID. Before archiving, save the person you chose to your iPhone Contacts. To make changes again, tap "Unarchive" at the bottom of the project or in Settings → Archived projects.

Deleting a contact or a project

To delete a contact, swipe left on it in the project and tap "Delete", or open the contact, tap edit, and choose "Delete contact". A whole project, together with its contacts, can be deleted from the project's menu. Deleting cannot be undone.

WhoKeep Pro

The free version includes up to two projects; archived projects count toward this limit. WhoKeep Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it unlocks unlimited projects and exporting projects as a spreadsheet. Contacts, notes, history, reminders, and Caller ID are free either way. If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, use "Restore purchase" in Settings.

My data disappeared

Your projects are stored only on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall WhoKeep. They may be restored together with the app from an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

Language

WhoKeep is available in English and Ukrainian and follows your iPhone's language setting.

Privacy

WhoKeep keeps everything on your iPhone and does not collect personal information. For details, read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

For additional help, contact the WhoKeep developer at car.tools.apps@gmail.com. When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, WhoKeep version, and a short description of what happened.
