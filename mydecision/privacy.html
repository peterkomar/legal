Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyDecision handles information when you use the application.

1. Information used by the app

MyDecision gives a random YES or NO answer when you tap the decision button. The app does not ask for your name, email address, account, or any other personal information, and it does not ask you to type in the question you are thinking about.

The only information the app keeps is your decision history: the sequence of YES and NO answers you have received. The history is used to show the YES/NO percentages and the decision history chart.

2. Local storage

The decision history is stored locally on your device. MyDecision does not operate servers, does not have user accounts, and does not upload the decision history or any other information to the developer.

Depending on your device settings, locally stored app data may be included in the backups made by your operating system, such as Android backup or iCloud and device backups on iOS. These backups are managed by Google or Apple according to their own terms and your settings, not by MyDecision.

3. Network access

MyDecision works entirely offline. The app does not request internet access permission on Android and does not connect to the internet or to other devices to provide its features.

4. Analytics and third-party services

MyDecision does not include analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs that collect usage information.

5. Advertising and tracking

MyDecision does not display advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

MyDecision does not sell or share personal information.

6. Permissions

MyDecision does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. The app uses the device's standard haptic feedback (vibration) when you make or clear a decision, which does not require a special permission.

7. Children's privacy

MyDecision does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Data retention and deletion

The decision history stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by tapping the "Clear" button in the app. For a few seconds after clearing, an "Undo" option lets you restore the history; after that, the history is permanently removed from the app.

All app data, including the decision history, is also removed when you clear MyDecision's application data where the platform supports it or when you uninstall the app. Copies included in operating-system backups are managed through your Google or Apple backup settings.

9. Security

Because the decision history never leaves your device through the app, it is protected by your device's own security features, such as the screen lock and the operating system's app data isolation.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MyDecision's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the MyDecision developer at car.tools.apps@gmail.com or using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play or App Store listing.
