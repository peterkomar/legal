MyDecision Support

Need help with MyDecision? Try the answers below or contact the developer.

How does MyDecision work?

Think of a yes-or-no question and tap the round play button. After a short "thinking" animation, the app shows a random YES or NO answer. Each answer is added to your decision history, where you can see the YES/NO percentages and a chart of your latest decisions.

Are the answers really random?

Yes. Every answer is chosen at random with an equal chance of YES and NO, and it does not depend on previous answers. MyDecision is meant for fun and for breaking a tie when you cannot choose; please do not rely on it for important decisions.

Why does the chart show only some of my decisions?

The chart shows your 30 most recent decisions so that it stays readable. The YES and NO percentages above the chart are always calculated from your whole history.

Clearing the history

Tap the "Clear" button to delete your decision history. If you cleared it by mistake, tap "Undo" in the message that appears for a few seconds after clearing. The "Clear" button is inactive while the history is empty.

My history disappeared

The history is stored only on your device. It is removed when you clear it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall MyDecision. It may be restored together with the app from your device's backup, depending on your Google or Apple backup settings.

Language

MyDecision is available in English and Ukrainian and follows your device's language setting.

Resetting the app

If MyDecision is not working as expected, close and reopen the app. You can also restart your device or uninstall and reinstall MyDecision. Reinstalling the app removes the locally stored decision history.

Privacy

MyDecision works offline and does not collect personal information. For details, read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

For additional help, contact the MyDecision developer at car.tools.apps@gmail.com. When reporting a problem, include your device model, Android or iOS version, MyDecision version, and a short description of what happened.
