Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PartyWheel handles information when you use the application.

1. Information used by the app

PartyWheel may ask you to provide a display name or nickname so that other participants can identify you during a game session.

2. Local storage

Information such as your local profile and application settings may be stored on your device. This information is used to provide the app's functionality.

3. Game rooms and participants

When you join or create a game room, information required for the game session, such as participant names and game state, may be shared with other devices participating in that room. This information is used to operate the multiplayer game session.

4. Questions and game content

PartyWheel includes predefined game questions and categories. This content is provided as part of the application.

5. Third-party services

PartyWheel does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's privacy

PartyWheel is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children. Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information as their display name or during gameplay.

7. Data deletion

Locally stored application data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling PartyWheel from the device. Session information may also be discarded when a game session ends.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PartyWheel's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the PartyWheel developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
