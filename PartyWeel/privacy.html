Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PartyWheel handles information when you use the application.

1. Information used by the app

PartyWheel may ask you to provide a display name or nickname so that other participants can identify you during a game session.

Your display name or nickname is stored locally on your device and may be shared with other devices participating in the same PartyWheel game room.

PartyWheel also uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect pseudonymous usage and technical information about how the application is used.

2. Local storage

Information such as your local player profile, nickname, avatar, and application settings may be stored on your device. This information is used to provide the app's functionality.

Locally stored profile and game information is not uploaded to PartyWheel-operated servers because PartyWheel does not currently operate a user-account or game-data backend.

3. Game rooms and participants

PartyWheel uses nearby device connections to allow players to participate in the same local multiplayer game.

When you create or join a game room, information required for the game session may be exchanged between participating devices. This may include participant names, avatars, game state, questions, reactions, scores, and other information required to synchronize the game.

This game-session information is used to provide the multiplayer experience between participating devices.

4. Questions and game content

PartyWheel includes predefined game questions, challenges, and categories. This content is provided as part of the application.

PartyWheel does not currently allow users to upload custom questions or game content to a PartyWheel-operated server.

5. Analytics and third-party services

PartyWheel uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the application is used and to improve its functionality, reliability, and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect technical and usage information, including app interactions, diagnostic information, device and app information, identifiers associated with the application installation, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID when available.

PartyWheel uses this information for analytics purposes, such as measuring how users interact with the application and understanding events such as room creation, room joining, game starts, and game completion.

PartyWheel does not intentionally send player nicknames, room names, room access codes, spoken answers, game answers, or other game-session content to Firebase Analytics.

Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's and Firebase's applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

6. Advertising and tracking

PartyWheel does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs to show ads.

The Android Advertising ID may be collected by Google Analytics for Firebase and used for analytics purposes.

PartyWheel does not use the Advertising ID to display targeted advertising and does not use Firebase Analytics for personalized advertising.

PartyWheel does not sell personal information.

7. Children's privacy

PartyWheel is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information as their display name or sharing sensitive information during gameplay.

PartyWheel does not intentionally request contact information, precise location, photos, voice recordings, or other sensitive personal information as part of normal gameplay.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application data can be removed by clearing PartyWheel's application data or uninstalling PartyWheel from the device.

Game-session information exchanged between nearby devices may be discarded when a multiplayer session ends or when the application is closed, depending on the type of information involved.

Analytics data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase is retained and managed according to Google's and Firebase's applicable data retention and deletion practices.

9. Security

PartyWheel uses Android platform services and nearby device communication technologies to operate multiplayer game sessions.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application and information transmitted between participating devices.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PartyWheel's features, analytics configuration, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the PartyWheel developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
