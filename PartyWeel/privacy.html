Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PartyWheel handles information when you use the application.

1. Information used by the app

PartyWheel may ask you to provide a display name or nickname so that other participants can identify you during a game session.

Your display name or nickname is stored locally on your device and may be shared with other devices participating in the same PartyWheel game room.

PartyWheel may also collect anonymous or pseudonymous usage events through Google Analytics for Firebase. These events may include actions such as creating a room, joining a room, starting a game, or completing a game.

2. Local storage

Information such as your local player profile, nickname, avatar, and application settings may be stored on your device. This information is used to provide the app's functionality.

Locally stored information is not uploaded to PartyWheel servers because PartyWheel does not currently operate a user-account or game-data backend.

3. Game rooms and participants

PartyWheel uses nearby device connections to allow players to participate in the same local multiplayer game.

When you create or join a game room, information required for the game session may be exchanged between participating devices. This may include participant names, avatars, game state, questions, reactions, scores, and other information required to synchronize the game.

This game-session information is used only to operate the multiplayer experience between participating devices.

4. Questions and game content

PartyWheel includes predefined game questions, challenges, and categories. This content is provided as part of the application.

PartyWheel does not currently allow users to submit custom questions or upload game content to a PartyWheel server.

5. Analytics and third-party services

PartyWheel uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the application is used and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect technical and usage information, including app interactions, device and app information, diagnostic information, and identifiers associated with the app installation.

PartyWheel uses analytics for aggregated usage measurement, such as understanding how often rooms are created, players join rooms, games are started, and games are completed.

PartyWheel does not use the Android Advertising ID for analytics. Advertising ID collection in Firebase Analytics is disabled.

PartyWheel does not intentionally send player nicknames, room names, room access codes, game answers, or other game-session content to Firebase Analytics.

Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's and Firebase's applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

6. Advertising and tracking

PartyWheel does not currently display advertising and does not use advertising SDKs.

PartyWheel does not use the Android Advertising ID for analytics or advertising purposes.

PartyWheel does not sell personal information and does not use Firebase Analytics for personalized advertising.

7. Children's privacy

PartyWheel is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information as their display name or during gameplay.

PartyWheel does not intentionally collect names, contact information, precise location, photos, voice recordings, or other sensitive personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application data can be removed by clearing PartyWheel's application data or uninstalling PartyWheel from the device.

Game-session information may be discarded when a multiplayer session ends or when the application is closed, depending on the type of data involved.

Analytics data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase is managed according to Google's and Firebase's applicable data retention and deletion practices.

9. Security

PartyWheel uses Android platform services and nearby device communication technologies to operate multiplayer game sessions.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data transmitted between participating devices and data processed by third-party services used by the application.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PartyWheel's features, analytics configuration, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the PartyWheel developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
