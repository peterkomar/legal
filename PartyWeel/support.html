PartyWheel Support

Need help with PartyWheel? Try the steps below or contact the developer through the PartyWheel App Store listing.

Creating or joining a game

Make sure all players have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled, keep their devices nearby, and allow PartyWheel to access the local network when prompted. One player should create a room before the others try to join it.

A game room does not appear

Confirm that PartyWheel is open on every device and that all players are using the latest version of the app. If the room still does not appear, close and reopen PartyWheel, then create a new room.

Changing or deleting a player profile

Open the profile editor in PartyWheel to change your nickname or avatar. Use the trash button in the profile editor to delete the locally stored profile.

Resetting the app

If PartyWheel is not working as expected, close and reopen the app. You can also restart your device or uninstall and reinstall PartyWheel. Reinstalling the app may remove locally stored profiles and settings.

Privacy

For details about how PartyWheel handles information, read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

For additional help, contact the PartyWheel developer at car.tools.apps@gmail.com. When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, PartyWheel version, and a short description of what happened.
